A new baby. A new chapter.

Get to know Jordan

Meet Jordan and baby Maya.

Jordan is 22 and became a mother for the first time four days ago. She recently moved to the community with her partner, Taylor, and is getting to know local services while recovering and caring for Maya.

During a previous healthcare visit, Jordan felt that her pain was dismissed before she had finished explaining it. That experience makes her worry she will not be taken seriously if she asks for help again.

What matters to Jordan

“I want someone to listen to my concerns and help me understand my options.”

Jordan wants to feel respected and included in decisions about her care. She values clear, practical information and having time to explain how she feels.

Her strengths

Jordan is curious and resourceful. She notices changes in her health, writes down questions, and looks for reliable information.

She and Taylor are learning together. Her aunt checks in regularly by phone and encourages Jordan to talk about what she needs.

Life at home

Taylor works irregular shifts, and their budget is tight. Most of Jordan’s close supports live elsewhere, and she has not yet found a regular family doctor or nurse practitioner.

Jordan has her maternity team’s discharge contact information, but is unsure which local services to use for different concerns. She is building her support network.

You’ll help Jordan decide what to do next.

Follow her through three situations involving physical recovery, communicating with healthcare professionals, and emotional health after birth. Consider both her concerns and what might make getting help easier or harder.
